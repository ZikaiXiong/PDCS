PDCS documents for COIN-OR onboarding
====================================

CSRO.txt is the official blank Contributor's Statement of Respect for Ownership.
CSOL.txt and OCL.txt are the two sections of the official DOL text. The unmodified
combined document is retained as DOL_OFFICIAL.txt. These are UNSIGNED templates,
not replacements for the signed documents already supplied by the user.

Template sources:
https://www.coin-or.org/management/forms/csro.txt
https://www.coin-or.org/management/forms/dol.txt
Submission instructions: https://www.coin-or.org/contributing/code/

Record reviewed during preparation:
- signed_CSRO.pdf: Zikai Xiong's signed individual CSRO.
- signed_DOL.pdf: PDCS CSOL signed by Zikai Xiong, naming four owners and the
  Apache License 2.0, plus Zikai Xiong's individual OCL.
The signed files remain outside this release in the user's private forms folder.
OWNERSHIP.txt records the declared names and the remaining reconciliation.

CSOL is mandatory for a new project. The contributor must have all owners'
permission. Individual OCL confirmations are strongly recommended by the
submission guidelines but not mandatory. CSRO is personal; one person's form
does not cover another contributor. A person whose CSRO is already on file does
not need to submit a duplicate. No filing receipt or prior CSRO record for the
other named authors was supplied for this preparation.

Review the forms and fill any remaining fields before signing or filing them.
Only the actual signatory should sign a statement. Keep signed forms separate
from the public source archive. Legal statements go to secretary@coin-or.org;
the source package and cover note go to submissions@coin-or.org.
COVER_EMAIL.txt is a draft for review. No email or legal statement was sent.
